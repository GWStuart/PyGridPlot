# User guide

How to use PyGridPlot, from a first program to every setting. Each page
stands on its own; the [API reference](reference.md) lists every parameter
and default, and the [philosophy](../philosophy.md) explains why the
library works the way it does.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): installing, the first program, the
   frame loop and quick mode.
2. [Examples](examples.md): every example program, with a picture of it.
3. [The plane](the-plane.md): creating a plane, setting up what it shows,
   axis titles and arrowheads, categories on an axis, converting
   coordinates, embedding a plane and using several.
4. [Interaction](interaction.md): the built-in pan and zoom controls,
   configuring them, and moving the view from your own code.
5. [Plotting](plotting.md): every plotting method at a glance, the drawing
   order, and how unchanged plots are reused. Then, by topic:
   - [Functions and calculus](functions.md): functions `y = f(x)`, their
     derivatives and integrals, Taylor polynomials, Fourier series and
     sequences;
   - [Curves](curves.md): parametric, polar and implicit curves, and
     contour lines;
   - [Regions and fields](regions-and-fields.md): areas between graphs,
     inequalities, and vector and slope fields;
   - [Data](data.md): scatter plots, error bars, lines and steps, bar
     charts, histograms, box and violin plots, and colour by value;
   - [Shapes, text and legends](shapes-and-text.md): polygons, arrows,
     text and legends.
6. [Draggable points and sliders](draggable-points.md): points the user
   drags, freely, along one axis or along a curve, and sliders for values.
7. [Logarithmic axes](log-axes.md): log-log and semi-log planes.
8. [Styles and themes](styles-and-themes.md): colours, reusable styles, the
   palette, themes and how numbers are written.
9. [Recipes](recipes.md): worked examples (mouse readouts, animation,
   legends, screenshots, ...), performance tips and troubleshooting.
10. [API reference](reference.md): everything `import pygridplot` provides.
